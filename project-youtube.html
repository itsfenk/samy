Project Overview

Strategy, tools, and the journey from zero

Starting from scratch, this project built a growing community of learners drawn to science, history, and global affairs — reaching 7,056 views and 284 hours of watch time through consistent, data-driven content.

Through careful audience analysis and iterative content strategy, expertise developed across the full production pipeline — from script to screen. The channel demonstrates the ability to craft compelling educational narratives that resonate with diverse geographic audiences, particularly Arabic-speaking learners across MENA.

  • 7,056 Total Views with strong organic reach across multiple markets
  • 284.1 Hours Watch Time demonstrating sustained audience retention
  • Top Video: 109 hours — "Climate Change: What If Global Warming Disappeared?"
  • 46.7% MENA Reach — Egypt, Algeria, Morocco, and Saudi Arabia
  • 100% positive community engagement and supportive feedback
